# Code of Conduct

BetterCatarman.org is built by volunteers for the people of Catarman. We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome and safe, whatever their age, background, language, ability, gender, sexual orientation, religion, politics or level of experience. That includes residents, translators, developers, and people reporting a correction for the first time.

This code applies in the GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews), in correction reports, and anywhere someone represents the project in public.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to GitHub or to coding.
- Respect every language on the site. English, Filipino and Waray are all equally welcome in issues and discussions.
- Disagree about facts by pointing to sources, not by attacking people.
- Accept correction gracefully. Getting a fact wrong is normal, and fixing it is the point of the project.
- Remember that the site is neutral. Keep campaigning, and praise or criticism of officials or parties, out of the project.

## What isn't acceptable

- Insults, slurs, mockery, or personal or political attacks.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including continuing to contact someone who asked you to stop.
- Unwelcome sexual language, images or attention.
- Sharing someone's private information, such as their address, phone number or workplace, without their permission. This includes residents and local officials.
- Deliberately adding false or misleading information to the site.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone else to do any of the above.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, email **conduct@bettercatarman.org**. Tell us what happened, where and when, and include links or screenshots if you can.

Reports are read only by the maintainer and are kept confidential. We won't share who reported something without their permission. You'll get a reply, usually within a week.

If the report is about the maintainer, say so in the email. You can also report a GitHub account directly to GitHub through its [reporting page](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## What happens next

The maintainer will look at what happened and decide what to do, starting with the least that solves the problem:

1. **A private word.** An explanation of what was wrong, and a request to stop.
2. **A warning.** A clear statement of what will happen if it continues.
3. **A temporary ban** from taking part in the project.
4. **A permanent ban**, for serious or repeated harm.

Comments, commits or issues that break this code may be edited, hidden or removed.
