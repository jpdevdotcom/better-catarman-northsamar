# Security Policy

BetterCatarman.org is a static website. It has no accounts, no logins, no database and no payments, and it doesn't store anything visitors type. Even so, we want to hear about anything that could put visitors at risk or let someone change what the site shows.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't open a public issue for a security problem.** Report it privately instead:

- Email **security@bettercatarman.org**, or
- Use GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/jpdevdotcom/better-catarman-northsamar/security/advisories/new) for this repository.

Please include:

- what the problem is and where (the page address or the file in this repository);
- the steps to reproduce it;
- what an attacker could do with it.

## What to expect

This is a volunteer project, so these are goals rather than promises:

- We'll reply within **7 days** to confirm we've received your report.
- We'll keep you updated while we work on a fix, and tell you when it's live.
- We'll credit you when it's fixed, unless you'd rather stay anonymous.

Please give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you share it publicly.

## What's in scope

- The live site at [bettercatarman.org](https://bettercatarman.org) and its `www` address
- The code in this repository

For example: cross-site scripting, a way to change what the site shows, exposed secrets or keys, or a dependency with a known vulnerability that affects the site.

## What's out of scope

- **Wrong or outdated information** on the site. That's a correction, not a security problem; use **Report a correction** on the page instead.
- Problems in services the site links to or loads, such as Google Maps, Google Analytics, Open-Meteo or catarman.gov.ph. Report those to whoever runs them.
- Denial-of-service attacks, spam, and social engineering.
- Reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated problem.

## Safe harbour

If you act in good faith, avoid harming visitors or their data, and don't disrupt the site, we won't take any action against you for your research.
